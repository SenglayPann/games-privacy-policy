Privacy Policy Peaks & Perils

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Peaks & Perils is a solitaire card game made by Cyteoqz (“we”). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

You need no account, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location. Your progress is saved only on your phone. With your permission, the game sends anonymous play statistics so we can make it better, and you can change your mind any time in Settings. If the game crashes, a technical crash report is sent so we can fix it. This version has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Saved on your phone

Your progress (your climb, your records and your settings) is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

Play statistics (only with your permission)

On first launch the game asks whether you agree to send play statistics. If you agree, it uses Unity Analytics, a service of Unity Technologies, to send how the game is played (for example when a run or a hand starts and ends, its score, which charms were offered and picked, and which settings changed), a random ID made for this installation (not your name, and not linked to your Google account), your phone model and system version, the game’s version and language, and your approximate region worked out from your internet connection (never your precise location).

We use this only to understand how the game is played and where it is too hard or confusing. If you say no, none of it is sent. You can change your answer in Settings (Play stats) at any time.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, freezes or hits an error, Unity Diagnostics (Unity Technologies) sends a technical report: the error, the phone model, the system and game versions, and performance information. We use it only to find and fix the problem. It does not include your name or contact details; if you said no to play statistics, Unity leaves out the identifiers it would otherwise use for analytics. Unity’s privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Who we share it with

Only Unity Technologies, which runs these services for us. We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires it. Information is encrypted on its way (HTTPS). We do not run servers of our own; Unity stores the statistics and reports, and we keep them only as long as they help us improve the game.

Your choices

Turn play statistics off any time in Settings. To delete what was sent from your phone, use “Delete my data” in Settings, or write to us: we ask Unity to delete the statistics linked to your installation’s ID. We answer every request within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Google Play

When you install the game, Google Play handles the download and may collect information under Google’s own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. If you take part in a test, Google shares your feedback with us if you send it through Google Play.

Children

The game is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children; if you believe a child’s information was sent, write to us and we will ask for it to be deleted.

Changes

If a future version adds features that collect information (for example ads or purchases), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: cyteoqz@gmail.com
