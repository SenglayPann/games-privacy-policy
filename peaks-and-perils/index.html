Privacy Policy Peaks & Perils

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Peaks & Perils is a solitaire card game made by Cyteoqz (“we”).

What we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no in-app purchases in this version.

Your progress (your current climb, your best score and your settings) is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Information sent by the game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may receive anonymous technical information about the device, such as its model, operating system and graphics chip, to help improve the engine. This information does not identify you. Unity’s own privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Google Play

When you install the game, Google Play handles the download and may collect information under Google’s own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. If you take part in a test, Google shares your feedback with us if you send it through Google Play.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that collect information (for example ads or analytics), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
